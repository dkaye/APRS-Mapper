APRS Map v1.13  ·  Marin Amateur Radio Society

APRS Map is a real-time position tracking application for Marin Amateur Radio Society (MARS) public-service events. It displays APRS tracker positions on a live map and allows participants to optionally share their own GPS location.

Frequently Asked Questions

The map is blank or not loading.

Check your internet connection. The app requires network access to fetch live tracker data and map tiles. If you are on a slow or intermittent connection, try switching to a stronger WiFi network or cellular signal and pull down to refresh.

I don't see any trackers on the map.

Trackers only appear when they are actively beaconing. Outside of an active event, or if no trackers have been heard in the past several minutes, the map may be empty. Contact your event coordinator to confirm an event is in progress and that trackers are configured and transmitting.

The Share Location button is not visible.

Mobile location sharing must be enabled by the event administrator for the current event. If you expect to see it and don't, ask your event coordinator to enable it in the admin settings.

I tapped Share Location but got a PIN error.

The PIN is set by the event administrator. Ask your event coordinator for the correct PIN for the current event.

My position isn't appearing on the map after I start sharing.

After joining, your first position update is sent within 60 seconds. Make sure location permission is granted for the app in your device Settings. On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → APRS Map and set it to Always or While Using the App. If you are indoors or in a poor GPS environment, the app may not have a fix yet.

The app is draining my battery while sharing.

Background GPS use does consume battery. The app uses the Wake Lock to keep the screen available and uploads your position approximately once per minute — both are intentional when sharing is active. Tap Stop Sharing when you no longer need to broadcast your position.

How do I stop sharing my location?

Open the drawer by tapping the gear icon (⚙), then tap Stop Sharing at the bottom of the drawer. Your session ends immediately and your position stops updating on the map.

Where does my shared location data go?

Your position is transmitted to the global APRS-IS network under an assigned amateur radio callsign. This makes it publicly visible on APRS network sites such as aprs.fi. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I use the app without an amateur radio license?

Yes. Viewing the map requires no license. The Share Location feature transmits under a callsign assigned and licensed to MARS; participants using it are operating under the club's third-party authorization for official public-service events.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email doug@rds.com with a description of the issue and, if applicable, the device model and iOS/Android version you are using.
