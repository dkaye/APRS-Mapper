Privacy Policy

Effective June 20, 2026  ·  APRS Map v1.13  ·  Marin Amateur Radio Society

This Privacy Policy describes how the APRS Map application and website (marsaprs.org) handle information when you use them. The app and website are operated by the Marin Amateur Radio Society (MARS) and maintained by Doug Kaye, K6DRK.

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate a real-time APRS tracker display. We do not sell data, serve advertising, or track you across other websites or apps.

1. Viewing the Map

When you open the map at marsaprs.org or in the APRS Map app, no account is required and no personal information is collected. Standard web server logs record your IP address and browser type as part of normal Apache operation. These logs are rotated and deleted after 7 days and are used only for diagnosing server problems.

The map loads tile images from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Your IP address is visible to those servers when tiles are fetched. See the OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy for details. All web traffic passes through Cloudflare, which provides DDoS protection and TLS termination; Cloudflare may log request metadata per their Privacy Policy.

2. Location Sharing

Location sharing is an optional feature available when enabled by the event administrator. You must actively choose to use it by tapping Share Location and entering an event PIN. It is never enabled automatically.

What we collect

  • Your name — entered by you when joining. Shown to other map viewers alongside your position marker.
  • Your GPS position — latitude and longitude, read from your device approximately once per minute while sharing is active.
  • A session token — a random value stored on the server to authenticate your updates. Not linked to any account or persistent identity.
APRS is a public protocol. When you share your location, your position is transmitted as a packet to the global APRS-IS network under an assigned amateur radio callsign (e.g., K6DRK-01). This data is publicly visible on external sites including aprs.fi and CalTopo, and may be retained by those services independently of MARS. Do not use Share Location if you do not want your position to be publicly visible.

How it is used

  • Your position is displayed on the MARS APRS event map so other participants and net control can see where you are.
  • Your name is shown in the tracker list and on your map marker.
  • No other use is made of this data.

Retention

  • Your session entry (name, token, last-update time) is automatically removed from the server after 24 hours of inactivity, or immediately when you tap Stop Sharing.
  • Your last 10 position fixes are kept in a history file for breadcrumb display. This history is cleared when you start a new session and is not retained after the event.
  • APRS-IS and third-party sites (aprs.fi, CalTopo) receive your position independently and retain it per their own policies, which are outside our control.

Stopping

Tap Stop Sharing at any time. Your session ends immediately, your position stops updating, and your entry is removed from the server. APRS-IS packets already transmitted are outside our control.

3. Device Permissions

The APRS Map app requests location permissions only when you use Share Location:

  • Location (While Using the App) — to read your GPS position for display on the map and to power the Share Location feature.
  • Location (Always / Background) — requested when you start sharing, so your position continues to be uploaded when the app is in the background. You can revoke this at any time in your device Settings; doing so stops all position collection immediately.

Location permission is never requested if you only view the map without using Share Location.

4. Event Administration

Authorized MARS personnel with admin access can view the names and callsigns of active sharing participants and can rename, block, or end participant sessions. Admin access is password-protected.

5. Children's Privacy

The APRS Map app and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information through this app, contact us at the address below and we will remove it promptly.

6. No Analytics or Advertising

We do not use analytics services, crash reporters, or advertising networks. No tracking cookies or persistent identifiers are set beyond the short-lived admin session cookie used for the password-protected admin pages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
