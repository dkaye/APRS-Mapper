Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Static Map app for iOS and Android.

Information We Collect

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Location Data

The app requests access to your device's location in order to display your current position on the map. Your location is used only within the app and is never transmitted to any server, stored persistently, or shared with any third party.

Map Tiles

On first launch, the app downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap (openstreetmap.org) and stores them locally on your device. These requests are standard tile requests that do not include any personally identifiable information. After the initial download, the app operates entirely offline and makes no further network requests.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Data Storage

All data (map tiles and temporary location data) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact
